# NURIVA Brand Guidelines

## Vision

To make healthcare feel compassionate, accessible, intelligent, and personal through technology.

---

## Mission

Connecting patients, doctors, caregivers, and AI into one unified healthcare ecosystem.

---

## Values

• Compassion
• Trust
• Accessibility
• Simplicity
• Innovation
• Privacy
• Safety

Name: Miso 🐱

Role:
Digital Healthcare Companion

Care.
